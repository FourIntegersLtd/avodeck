What each role can do

Owner and admin: add and remove people, see every case's details, dates and invoices. They can't open the medical records unless they're added to that case. Expert: their own cases. Only the named expert checks facts, writes the opinion and signs. Assistant: uploads, drafts emails and invoices, and reads, on cases they're added to. Can't check facts or sign. Viewer: reads cases they're added to.

When someone leaves

They lose access to Northgate's cases straight away, except cases where they are the named expert. Those stay with them for as long as the case is kept, because the duty to the court is theirs. Before a report is served, you can move a case to another expert. They start their own reading and checking from the beginning. Every organisation keeps at least one owner.
