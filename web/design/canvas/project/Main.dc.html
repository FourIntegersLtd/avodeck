Avodeck

Design system

Calm, visual and built for late-career clinicians. Green means go or done. Amber means please look. Nothing is hidden, nothing is tiny.

Six principles

01One thing per screenA single green button, always bottom right, worded as the next step.
02Show, don't listPictures of the bundle and the record. Numbers always come with words.
03Paper firstThe record page is the biggest thing on screen. Our findings sit beside it.
04Plain wordsDoctor to doctor. No software or legal-tech terms on screen.
05You can't break itSaves by itself, everything can be undone, no surprise pop-ups.
06Guided, not trappedThe next step is always shown. Gates say exactly what's left.
