# Deferred decisions

Things we chose not to build yet, and what would make us build them. Review at every phase gate (architecture 10.14). When a trigger fires, write an ADR and a slice brief.

| Deferred | Why not now | Trigger to build |
|---|---|---|
| SSO / SAML for organisations | Few team customers at launch | First team customer with more than 10 seats asks for it |
| Machine translation of foreign-language pages | Court needs certified translations; risk of mistranslation | A jurisdiction pack in a bilingual country (e.g. Quebec) or repeated requests from experts |
| Redaction tools | The bundle is evidence and kept as received | An instructing party requests redacted extracts as standard |
| Gmail/Outlook sending | `mailto:` is enough for the MVP | Experts report more than 5 minutes a case on emails |
| Cross-cell organisations | Data must stay in one cell | An organisation operating in two countries signs up |
| Exhibits analysis (audio, video, DICOM) | Out of intended purpose | A specialty pack needs it and the intended-purpose review allows it |
| Stripe subscription and its webhook (`POST /webhooks/stripe`, seats per organisation; slice O) | No Stripe account yet; organisations, members and invites work without billing | Stripe account opened |
| Seat-based billing beyond Stripe quantity | Simple per-seat subscription suffices | Agencies ask for pooled credits or per-case billing |
| Real-time co-editing of opinions | Opinions are personal to the named expert; ETag prevents loss | A joint-report workflow needs simultaneous editing |
| Automatic reprocessing with new models | Must never change verified work silently | Never automatically; opt-in only (10.3) |
| Slice J (examination and prognosis) | Not needed for breach of duty | First causation, condition-and-prognosis or IME pack |
| Offline editing (beyond witness-box search) | Complexity and conflict risk | Experts working regularly without connection |
| IAM Roles Anywhere for App Platform → AWS | Scoped, rotated access keys are simpler to run alone | Before the first paying customer, or if a security review asks for short-lived credentials |
| T-10 second reading of a typed-out page (slice B) | The AI kit sends text only, and identifier masking on page images (M9) is not built | Foundation adds image input to `core/ai/kit.py` with M9 masking |
| Marking a page as a copy from screen 10 (`duplicate_of` on `PATCH …/pages/{page_no}`) | Slice A's copy review (`PATCH …/pages/{page_id}/duplicate`) already does it with history | Experts ask to mark copies while typing pages out |
| Embedding pre-ranking for unused facts (T-14b) | The cell has no in-region embedder yet; the model ranks the pack-ordered candidates directly | A case has more unused citable facts per question than the model call can take, or the in-region embedder lands (stage 2) |
| Retrieval of facts for conflicting accounts at scale (T-14c) | Every citable fact of the case goes into one call, which fits the launch bundles | A case's citable facts exceed one model call, or the in-region embedder lands (stage 2) |
| Warning when the expert rejects a fact already cited in an opinion (failure mode F14, slice B with E) | Opinions and their citations belong to slice E and `core/contracts` has no "is this fact cited, and where" contract; slice B must not read E's tables | E citation contract: foundation adds a `core/contracts` function listing the opinion paragraphs citing a fact, then B warns and asks for confirmation before `not_right` on a checked fact |
| Page-read log export as CSV with its hash chain (slice B's definition of done) | The export belongs to slice H's case export; slice B keeps the chain (`page_reads`, checked by `app.page_reads_chain_ok`) but `core/contracts/reading.py` has no way yet to hand the rows out | Slice H builds the case export (S26): foundation adds a page-read log read-out (rows with `seq`, `prev_hash`, `hash`) to `core/contracts/reading.py`, B implements it, H writes the CSV |
| Dictation on screen 10 (typing out an unreadable page by voice, `CouldntRead.tsx`) | The speech relay (Azure Speech over FastAPI WebSocket) is not built in foundation yet; typing works now | Foundation's speech relay lands, or experts ask to dictate transcriptions |
| A better reranker, and a link-recall floor of 0.8 for T-11 (slice D) | The cell reranks with `MiniLM-L-6-v2` int8 in-process (ADR 0029), but on the synthetic bundle it does not beat the embedder alone (recall@10 0.55 against 0.58). The T-11 floor is **recall@10 ≥ 0.5** (`app/evals/links.py` `FLOOR`; stand-in 0.43, bge-small 0.58 on 10 questions), not the 0.8 first proposed: with ten questions and a few expected facts each the score moves 0.1 per fact | A larger links ground truth (more bundles and questions), or a domain-tuned reranker, or T-11 recall falls below 0.5; then raise the floor towards 0.8 |
| The expert's own checks, T-13b (`POST /v1/me/checks/custom`, slice D) | Pack checks cover the launch specialty; a rule builder needs its own review of rule 1 | Experts ask for checks the packs don't have; the route returns 501 `not_built` until then |
| Dates of birth for identifier stripping in T-11 and T-13a (slice D) | No slice records the person's date of birth on the case yet; the `dates_of_birth` seam in `understand/services.py` defaults to none and `pii.strip` still removes the pack's identifier patterns | Slice C (or foundation) records the date of birth and `core/contracts` offers a read of it; slice D wires the seam to it |
| Turning a link `stale` in the database when its fact is rejected (slice D with B) | Slice D may not hook slice B's fact changes; the link reads as `stale` in `/fact-links` but the row stays `confirmed` | Foundation keeps `confirmed_links` to checked facts, or adds a fact-rejected event slices can act on |
| Rate limits per person, organisation and endpoint (architecture 10.9) | Only sign-in codes and spot-check links (per address, migration 0607) are limited; the launch has a handful of invited experts | Before the pilot opens to outside experts |
| Yearly KMS key rotation and data-key re-wrap (10.9) | The cell has no KMS key yet (no cloud accounts) | The cell's KMS key is created (stage 2); rotation is set when it is |
| `mandatory_update` pack corrections blocking signing (10.11) | Signing belongs to slice F, not built yet | Slice F builds signing: it refuses to sign while the case has an unacknowledged mandatory pack correction |
| Approving the launch packs (`status: approved`, `approved_by`, `approved_on`) | The loader refuses unapproved packs when `PACKS_APPROVED_ONLY=1`, but no named legal or clinical reviewer has signed the launch packs off | Before the first production cell: the founder names the reviewers, and the production cell sets `PACKS_APPROVED_ONLY=1` |
| E-signature provider for joint statements | No provider chosen; send-for-signature returns the prepared statement and a signing reference, and a copy signed elsewhere is uploaded | A provider is chosen (decision record) and its webhook is wired to the mounted route |
| Slice G links for the trial slice (hearings, served written answers, `report_changes` row on a changed position) | Slice G is not on main; `trial/ports.py` stands in | G's contracts land in `core/contracts`; replace the stand-ins |
| Recording the instructing party's consent after a replacement case is made (S3) | `case_replacements.instructing_party_consented` is set once, when slice O creates the replacement; consent that arrives later needs a new replacement or a foundation function | Slice A builds the bundle copy and consent often arrives after the case is made |
| The date an outgoing expert gave a fact, on reassignment (S3) | The reset (migration 0031) restores an edited fact's value, fields, words and box from the verification log, but the log keeps no `event_date`, so a date the outgoing expert typed or resolved stays on the suggestion | The verification log gains old and new dates (slice B), or experts report a carried-over date |
| Auditing each look by a records custodian | Custodians only read served cases transferred by support (migration 0032); unlike support, their views are not written to the audit trail one by one | The legal check on custody (ADR 0012) asks for it |
| Copying the bundle into a replacement case (scenario S3, `POST /cases/{case_id}/replacement`; slice O with A) | The bundle is slice A's; copying it needs the instructing party's recorded consent and an A contract to copy pages with their numbers and OCR. The replacement case itself is made (slice O, `core/contracts/replacement.py`) without the bundle | Slice A takes the copy: a `core/contracts` call that copies a bundle into a case only when consent is recorded |
