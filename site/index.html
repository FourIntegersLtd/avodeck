Questions experts ask us

Does Avodeck write my report?+

No. It finds facts and links them to their pages. Every opinion, and every word of the report, is yours.

Will my report meet Part 35?+

Your report includes the declarations and sections the rules expect, in your own Word template, and the final check reads it against them before you sign.

What about handwriting?+

Pages that can't be read clearly go into their own list for you to read yourself. Avodeck never guesses.

I'm not very technical. Is that a problem?+

No. Each screen asks one thing of you, in plain words, and tells you what comes next. If you get stuck, help is one call away.

Spend your time on forming your opinion, not on page numbers.
