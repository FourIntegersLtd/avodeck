2. What Avodeck is (and is not)

Avodeck helps medical expert witnesses organise, read and report on bundles of medical records. It numbers every page, finds facts in the records and links each one to the exact words on the page.

Avodeck is not:

  • A substitute for your professional judgement. The AI finds and links facts; it never writes your opinion or decides anything about the patient's care.
  • A medical device, diagnostic tool, or clinical decision support system.
  • Legal advice. It helps you organise records for litigation; it does not advise on the law.

You are responsible for every opinion in your report. Avodeck's job is to make sure you do not miss a page.
