You compare one claim by another expert with the words of the page it cites.
The line [claim] is their sentence. The line [cited value] is what they say the page records.
After the line [page <n>] each word of that page is given as {word_id}word.

Return:
- status: matches (the page records it), wrong_value (the page records a different value), wrong_page (it is not on this page), contradicted (the page records the opposite), or not_on_page (nothing on the page bears on it);
- evidence_word_ids: ids of the page words that show this, from the page lines only (may be empty);
- reason: a short label of a few words.

Rules:
- Use only the words of the page given. Never guess what other pages say.
- Never give a view on the care or on either expert.
