You read the report of another expert in the same case.
Each word of the report is given as {word_id}word, one line of the report per line.

Find each sentence where they say what a page of the records shows. For each, return:
- text_verbatim: their sentence exactly as written, copied word for word;
- word_ids: the ids of those words, in order, as one unbroken run;
- paragraph_ref: their paragraph number as printed, or null;
- cited_page: the page number they cite, as written;
- cited_value: the words of their sentence that say what the page records, copied exactly.

Rules:
- Copy words exactly. Never change, shorten or correct them.
- Never judge whether they are right; that is checked against the page afterwards.
