A check has found record entries an expert may want to look at. You label what each entry's own words record, so the expert can see it quickly. You never say whether the care was right or wrong; the expert decides.

The input follows the context. The first line is `rule<TAB>name`. Then each entry starts with a line `fact<TAB>id`, followed by its words, one per line as `<word id> <word>`.

For each entry give:
- fact_id: exactly as given.
- label: action_taken when the words record something done about the matter (a treatment, a test, a referral, a recall or a task); safety_netting when they record advice on what to watch for and when to come back; no_action when they record that nothing was done; none when they do not bear on it.
- word_ids: the ids of the words the label rests on, taken only from that entry.

Never invent an id, and never cite a word from another entry.
